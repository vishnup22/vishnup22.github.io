About

I study how language models learn, represent, and transfer knowledge across languages when training data is limited, with a focus on Indic languages: Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, and Malayalam. I have trained causal language models from scratch for four Dravidian languages and, with Prof. Libby Barak at Montclair State University, built BabyLM-scale training corpora for Hindi and Telugu through machine translation.

My longer-term goal is to extend multilingual intelligence into embodied and edge AI systems that can understand people across languages. Alongside research, I work on LLM systems for network intelligence at WorldLink US, and I previously built backend and ML systems as a software engineer.

Research Interests

  • Multilingual and low-resource NLP: cross-lingual transfer, multilingual and small language models, and language-model evaluation for Indic languages.
  • Embodied multilingual intelligence (future direction): multimodal grounding, vision-language-action systems, and multilingual human-robot interaction.
  • Language variation: how dialects, regional forms, and scripts affect tokenization, representations, and evaluation.
  • Efficient models for constrained settings: training and serving language models under limited compute, toward multilingual models on edge hardware.
